What is BitLife?

BitLife is a text-based life simulator. You're born into a random family, in a random city, with your own looks, smarts and health — then you live your whole life one year at a time. Every tap of Age moves you forward, and every choice along the way (study hard or skip class, chase a dream job or play it safe, fall in love or stay single) shapes who you become.

This page lets you play BitLife Unblocked straight in the browser. It runs on GitHub Pages, so it's lightweight and loads quickly on school and work networks where app stores are blocked. Nothing to install — just start a new life.

How to play BitLife online

  1. Start a new life. Choose a random life or create your own character with a name, gender and country.
  2. Age up. Tap the big Age button to move forward one year. Events pop up as you grow — decide how you react.
  3. Use the menus. Occupation for school and jobs, Assets for money and property, Relationships for family, friends and partners, and Activities for everything else.
  4. Watch your stats. Your choices raise or lower your four core bars. Keep them healthy to live longer.
  5. Finish your life. When your character dies you get a summary of the life you lived. Then start again and try a different path.

The four BitLife stats

😊 Happiness

Up with good relationships, hobbies and vacations; down with stress, debt and bad news.

❤️ Health

The gym, doctor visits and a calm life help. Risky choices and old age wear it down.

🧠 Smarts

Study, read and visit the library. Smarts open the door to college and better jobs.

✨ Looks

Partly luck at birth, partly how you take care of yourself over the years.

BitLife tips for a better life

  • 📚 Study earlyGoing to the library and studying harder while you're young pays off with college and higher-paying careers.
  • ⚖️ Keep a balanceA big salary with zero happiness is still a rough life. Spend time with family and friends.
  • 💰 Save before you spendHouses, cars and luxury items cost money every year. Don't buy more than your income can handle.
  • 🩺 See the doctorIgnoring an illness can drain your health fast, especially as you get older.
  • 🎲 ExperimentThe fun of BitLife is replaying it. Try a perfect life, a chaotic life, a famous life — every run is different.
