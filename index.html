What is BitLife?

BitLife is a text-based life simulator. You are born into a random family, in a random city, with your own looks, smarts and health — then you live your whole life one year at a time. Every tap of Age moves you forward, and every choice you make along the way (study hard or skip class, chase a dream job or play it safe, fall in love or stay single) shapes the person you become.

This page lets you play BitLife Unblocked straight in the browser. Because it runs on GitHub Pages, it is lightweight and loads quickly on school and work networks where app stores are blocked. There's nothing to install: press Play and start a new life.

How to play BitLife online

  1. Start a new life. Press Play, then choose a random life or create your own character with a name, gender and country.
  2. Age up. Tap the big Age button to move forward one year. Events pop up as you grow — decide how you react.
  3. Use the menus. Open Occupation for school and jobs, Assets for money and property, Relationships for family, friends and partners, and Activities for everything else.
  4. Watch your stats. Your choices raise or lower your four core bars. Keep them healthy to live longer.
  5. Finish your life. When your character dies you get a summary of the life you lived. Then start again and try a completely different path.

The four BitLife stats

😊 HappinessRaised by good relationships, hobbies and vacations; lowered by stress, debt and bad news.
❤️ HealthGym, doctor visits and a calm life help. Risky choices and old age wear it down.
🧠 SmartsStudy, read books and go to the library. Smarts open doors to college and better jobs.
✨ LooksPartly luck at birth, partly how you take care of yourself over the years.

BitLife tips for a better life

  • Study early. Going to the library and studying harder at school while you're young pays off later with college and higher-paying careers.
  • Keep a balance. A high salary with zero happiness is still a rough life. Spend time with family and friends, and take breaks.
  • Save money before you spend it. Houses, cars and luxury items cost money every year. Don't buy more than your income can handle.
  • Visit the doctor when you're sick. Ignoring an illness can drain your health fast, especially as you get older.
  • Experiment. The fun of BitLife is replaying it. Try a perfect life, a chaotic life, a famous life — each run is different.
